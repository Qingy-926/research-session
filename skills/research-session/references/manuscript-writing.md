# Research manuscript writing and acceptance

For drafting, revising, and finalizing papers, research reviews, and research reports. Coursework reports are outside this skill. This reference adds writing decisions; it does not duplicate execution protocols or authorize experiments.

## Define this delivery

- Local polishing: work within supplied text, preserving numbers, variables, citations, research position, and strength of certainty. Make the actual edits; missing README, Git, or preregistration must not block independent wording work. Polishing does not prove scientific correctness.
- Content revision: locate the current draft, relevant data, and literature; revise reasoning and text and verify affected claims. If only review is requested, return concrete issues and remedies. If modification or improvement is requested, update the authorized original, not merely suggestions or a few examples.
- Formal external research finalization: complete this reference's checks and [independent review](independent-review.md) point 3. Local editing is not a new complete submission review; repeated “polishing only” cannot bypass the final gate. A changed controlled version or dependency cannot inherit an old pass.

Follow established journal/school templates and author terminology. Ask under global authorization rules when an undecided audience, language, argument, or structural preference would materially change the product; make ordinary transitions and edits preserving scientific meaning independently. Do not silently change sample definitions, statistics, selection, causal interpretation, or research scope through polishing.

## What belongs in the manuscript

Build the narrative around the research question. A paragraph connects necessary evidence, reasoning, and qualifications for its point; vary length and syntax with the argument instead of mechanically announcing and summarizing each paragraph. Comparisons identify objects, conditions, specific differences, and significance. Lists or tables suit methods and genuinely parallel conditions/comparisons; otherwise use connected prose.

Use precise discipline terminology and concrete nouns and verbs, retaining the author's valid conventions. Remove background, repetition, and self-assessment that add no information. Ground broad claims and contrastive phrases in actual relationships and evidence or remove them; these are not banned words. Keep real comparisons, necessary technical expression, and active/passive voice as appropriate.

The manuscript explains the research. Put production notes and collaboration requests in a separate delivery note, including tools, attachment readability, or requested user materials. Keep limitations, untested scope, and uncertainty affecting conclusions in the text. Do not disguise internal evidence placeholders as a submission-ready final. Do not invent personal experiences or manufacture naturalness by removing technical substance or hiding evidence gaps.

For example, if only development set D, seed=23, EER 4.3% is available and no independent test was done, one may state that result and that independent validation is pending. If cross-dataset validity is a claim, explicitly state the missing evidence; do not claim generalization is proven. These numbers illustrate wording only and must not enter a real paper.

## Acceptance after revision

Scale checks to the change; do not upgrade every punctuation edit into a full audit.

1. **Argument:** does it answer the research question? For cross-section changes, align the introduction gap/objective, results, discussion contributions, and conclusion; do not add unsupported claims in the conclusion.
2. **Facts:** align variables, sample sizes, split/seed, metric definitions and denominators, figure/table numbers, and text values. Trace results to raw evidence; distinguish observations, mechanistic hypotheses, and causal judgments. Do not make unsupported affirmative generalizations from a negative or single result.
3. **References:** internally classify as source-verified, awaiting external verification, or unconfirmed. Check that references support their claims; do not invent authors, dates, titles, DOI, or pages. Retain citations required for formal papers; do not import coursework rules that add sources only on request.
4. **Artifact:** actually read final text and check semantic changes, terminology, formulas, figures, tables, and necessary layout. Keep production notes out of the manuscript. Use document tools for Word/PDF production and actual rendering when needed.

Keep verification entry points and gaps. Fix issues within authorization before delivery. When key facts or the finalization gate are unmet, accurately label the output draft, polished passage, or pending verification, not submission-ready final; continue independent parts.

## Multiple agents and course correction

Different chapters, citations, and statistical review can be divided only when current host and authorization permit. The main session checks originals and merges duplicate/conflicting feedback, distinguishing required changes, suggestions, and acceptable material. Subtask opinions do not replace data or fixed §6 reviews. Follow client model selection rules; do not change the specified model merely for independence.

When new instructions arrive, keep still-valid extraction, data preparation, and text; redo only affected parts. Check whether the research population, inclusion, variable definitions, or evidence basis changes. Obtain a decision before changing scientific meaning; restructuring categories does not authorize rerunning experiments.
