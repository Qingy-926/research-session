# Height: objectives and overall route

## Original user prompt

> 我接下来需要你帮我完成：【任务】。请不要只满足于表面填空。请帮我：1、找出这个任务背后的终极目标；2、设计一个能把人类解放出来的更高级、自动化方案；3、挑出当前最急需决定的3个决策方向，并列出正反论点。

The original label was “思考的高度（改目标不改任务）”. Apply it as an exploration of higher-level goals and alternative routes, not as permission for AI to change the confirmed objective.

## Adapted prompt

```text
I need to complete [task]. Reconsider it at the top level without changing confirmed boundaries:
1. Separate the explicit task, inferred higher-level objective, and success criteria; identify assumptions.
2. Compare the current route with approaches that reduce manual work, including automation benefits, costs, human control points, and failure handling. Automation is not necessarily better than a simple process.
3. Present at most three truly urgent decisions with arguments for and against, key evidence gaps, and recommendations; do not invent decisions to reach three.
Continue authorized work. Treat proposals outside the objective, budget, protocol, or environment as options requiring a decision, not deployments.
```

## Applicability and exit

Use for choosing an overall route, identifying a real bottleneck, or improving repetitive manual workflows. For a clear isolated fix, frozen plan, or artifact format, complete the original task without reframing its objective.

Deliver a higher-level objective, comparable routes, and key tradeoffs; a complete automation system is not required every time. With insufficient evidence, offer conditional routes rather than calling speculation the user's true ultimate goal.
